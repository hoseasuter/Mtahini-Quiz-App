Created MTAHINI QUIZ APP as my final Python Essentials course Project.
MTAHINI QUIZ APP is a desktop-based exam administration and automated grading system designed to replace the traditional pen-and-paper exam process. It solves two core problems: 1.Exam Delivery & Grading — Students log in with their ID and take a timed multiple-choice quiz. The app automatically grades every answer, eliminating manual marking errors and saving the teacher's time. 2.Instant Performance Feedback — After submitting, students immediately see their score, pass/fail status, and a detailed question-by-question breakdown (correct, wrong, or skipped) — no waiting for results.They can also export the peformance report to their computer for futere reference. In short: It digitizes the full exam lifecycle — from taking the test and to grading it all in one self-contained app with no internet required.
